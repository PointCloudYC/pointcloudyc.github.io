About Me

I am a Research Associate at HKUST. From 2022 to 2026, I was an Associate Researcher at the Geography Institute of Guangzhou, Guangdong Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. from HKUST, where I worked with Prof. Jack Cheng on point cloud processing, deep learning, and BIM modeling from LiDAR data.

My research focuses on 3D point cloud processing, foundation models such as SAM, DINO, and CLIP, multi-modal 2D/3D LLMs, semantic and instance segmentation, weakly-supervised and long-tailed learning, large-scale dataset construction, and remote sensing and GIS applications.
